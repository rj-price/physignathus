The falcon bees

The nightmare of the giant locusts

The falcon bees are the already quite strange decendants of the vulture bees first seeded upon the planet of Physignathus which have taken advantage of the numerous giant locust plagues. The giant locusts first evolved around 4600 years PE and have since radiated into many different clades in just a few million years, ravenging the young world as they did. But as you have been told already their sucsess was short and only one and half a million years ago a new preditor has evolved to control the population. These are the falcon bees.

The falcon bees are suffering the same fate as their prey though as they are eating their food faster than it can be replenished. The falcon bees are another fleeting moment on this new planet, a survivor who have just taken advantage of the unstable ecosystem. Although there seems no hope for these massive predtory bees, some are adapting to other sources of food becoming smaller and more nymble as they do so. These smaller falcon bees have adapted to eat the less common but still plentifull beatles which scurry across the floor.

The future of the falcon bees seems unceartain though one thing is ceartain the giant locusts and their preditors are doomed to extinction.
