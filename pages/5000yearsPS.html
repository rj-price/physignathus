We won't talk too mauch about them as they already have their own page but we shall talk about others of this time. Like the primary scavengers of Physignathus as of 5000 years PE, the decendants of the vulture bees which have become extremely specialised for scavenging. These bees have created massive supercolonies which swarm carrcases and devour them. Other bees have become flightless and ant like, while others still have become solitary hunters. The vertibrates are still thriving though and most of the lizards seeded have grown larger and fatter with no preditors to cull the population. Draco lizards have also dominated the new bamboo forests and have two distinct species the ground draco and the tree draco. The ground draco has adapted, surprisingly, for life on the floor and have become large and flightless and the tree draco has become smaller and more nymble for a life in the canopy.
