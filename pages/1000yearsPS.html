1000 years post seeding

The first thousand years are of extereme growth. The first to dominate this new planet was grass. Grass quickly spread accross the globe covering every continent in a carpet of green, forming vast grassland habitats. Which prompted the explosion of dragon population across the planet with green water dragons being most common. Then came the bammbo which quickly created the first forests on Physignathus and the yuccas spreading across the desert, these new habitats causes another rise in population for the other two dragons. It is at this time which early specialization occurs in the dragons with subtle differences alowing them to eat certain foods.

The oceans, however, arent fairing well in the early life of Physignathus. Large algal mats grow to sizes not seen since the boring billion and in doing so suffocating the life underneath. Most water animals then fled to the inland freshwater environments waiting in lakes and rivers for the algae to pass.
